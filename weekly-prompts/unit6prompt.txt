Chapter 13 Topic(s)

"Collisions are checked between the spaceship and aliens to enable the game ending.
What is the sprite utility used? What is the difference between that and the groupcollide in the previous question?"

The sprite utility used to check the game-ending collision is pygame.sprite.spritecollideany().
The difference between the groupcollide() and spritecollideany() are primarily between what they accept and the data they return.
Spritecollideany takes a single sprite (The Ship) and checks it against an entire group of sprites (The Aliens). 
It loops through the entire group until it finds a member that has collided against the sprite, and returns that alien. Otherwise, it returns none.
On the other hand, groupcollide compares two entire groups of sprites against each other, the bullets and the aliens.
Instead of just returning the sprite and stopping after a hit, it evaluates every member of both groups.
When the rect of the bullet connects with the rect of the alien, groupcollide adds a key-value pair to a dict.
Groupcollide makes the alien disappear right when it gets hit via boolean arguments, while spritecollideany reports the impact, letting you decide the outcome of the impact.
